Privacy Policy

Effective 13 October 2026 · AveryDay is in beta

AveryDay ("we") is a habit tracker run by an independent developer. This policy says what we collect, why, who helps us process it, how long we keep it, and what you can do about it. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking tools.

What we collect

DataWhy
Account: email address, name, password (stored only as a one-way hash), time zone, whether your email is verified, your planTo create and secure your account, and to decide what "today" is for your streaks and reminders
Google sign-in (if you use it): your Google account ID, email and nameTo sign you in. We never see your Google password.
What you enter: habits, check-ins and notes, goals, resources (notes and links), reminder times, rest daysThis is the service: tracking, streaks, levels, the dashboard
Notifications: your in-app notifications and, if you turn on push, your browser's push subscription (an address at your browser's push service, plus keys)To send the reminders you set
Sign-in sessions: a session token (stored hashed) and its expiryTo keep you signed in, and to sign you out everywhere after a password change or reset
Technical data: IP address and request details, in short-lived server logs and in memory for rate limitsSecurity (stopping password guessing and abuse) and fixing errors
Email preference: whether you agreed to occasional product emails (off unless you turn it on)To respect your choice. Account emails such as verification and password reset are always sent.

We don't collect payment details: there are no payments in AveryDay today. Please don't put sensitive information (health details, for example) in habit names or notes unless you're comfortable storing it with us.

Our legal bases

  • Contract: to provide the account and the service you signed up for.
  • Consent: push notifications and product emails. You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings or in your browser.
  • Legitimate interest: keeping the service secure and working, such as rate limits, logs and backups.

Who processes data for us

We use these providers to run AveryDay. Each handles data only to provide its service to us. Your data may be stored or processed outside your country.

  • Vercel serves the web app and passes your requests to our server.
  • Render runs our server.
  • Supabase hosts our database.
  • Google provides "Sign in with Google" (if you use it) and Gmail, which sends our emails. Google sees the recipient address and the message.
  • Your browser's push service (for example Google for Chrome, Mozilla for Firefox, Apple for Safari) delivers push reminders. The reminder text is encrypted on our server, and only your browser can read it.
  • GitHub stores our encrypted database backups (see below).

Cookies and storage in your browser

We use one essential cookie: a secure, HttpOnly sign-in cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 7 days. The app also stores a few preferences in your browser's local storage (your light or dark theme, and the last level you saw). No advertising or analytics cookies. If you use "Sign in with Google", Google's sign-in script loads from Google and is covered by Google's privacy policy.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and everything in it: until you delete your account.
  • When you delete your account (Settings → Delete account), your account and all its data are removed from our database right away.
  • Backups: we take an encrypted backup of the database every day and keep each one for 30 days, so deleted data disappears from backups within 30 days.
  • Password-reset links expire after 30 minutes and email-verification links after 24 hours; expired ones are deleted within about two days. Sign-in sessions expire after 7 days.
  • Server logs: kept by our hosting provider for a short period (days to weeks), then deleted.

Your rights

  • Access and portability: Settings → Download my data gives you everything as a JSON file.
  • Correction: edit your name, time zone and preferences in Settings, and your habits and goals in the app.
  • Deletion: Settings → Delete account. It can't be undone.
  • Objection and withdrawing consent: turn off push or product emails at any time.
  • You can contact us about any of these, and you may complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Passwords are hashed with BCrypt. Session and reset tokens are stored only as hashes. Backups are encrypted. Access to the database is limited to the developer. We look at an individual account's content only when you ask for help, or to investigate a fault or abuse.

Children

AveryDay is not meant for children under 13, or under the minimum age for digital consent where you live. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
